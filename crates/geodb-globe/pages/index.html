URL parameters

  • ?webgl2 forces the WebGL2 build, even where WebGPU works.
  • ?load=cities loads the cities at start (else on demand); ?layers=coords,meta,names,fold,foldhan,foldhangul loads data layers (with the cities) at start. ?positions=exact queries on exact coordinates instead of geoids.
  • ?imagery=4k,8k,16k and ?lines=10m load the Earth detail layers. ?show= and ?compare= pick what is shown and what the split view compares it with.
  • ?tiles=osm|landsat|bluemarble|modis|modis-aqua|viirs turns on detail tiles; &date=YYYY-MM-DD picks the day for the daily ones.
  • ?at=lat,lon,distance sets the view (distance 1 is the surface).

Without tiles everything comes from this site's own files. With tiles on, the page fetches them from NASA GIBS or OpenStreetMap while you move.
